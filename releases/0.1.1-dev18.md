launcher-console isolation and focused diagnostics from dev16/dev17. Neither earlier tag
published a downloadable release because the new control-input fixture failed Windows CI.

and panic location without logging command text or panic payloads. Physical Explorer drops
and the original OpenCode background-server interaction require human retesting. Ctrl+Z keeps
the child program's normal Windows semantics. Tab detachment remains roadmap work.

passed, including the corrected control-input fixture and all six ConPTY tests. Hosted
